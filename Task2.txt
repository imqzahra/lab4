true .

Explanation:
This query checks for the existence of a male. The underscore is an anonymous variable, and the result is true because the knowledge base contains male facts.

Explanation:
This query follows two parent relationships to find Faisal's grandparent. Ali is the parent of Muhammad, who is the parent of Faisal.


5 ?- parent(ahmad,X), female(X).

Explanation:
This query looks for Ahmad's children who also satisfy the female predicate. Prolog identifies Sara and Nora.


6 ?- parent(X,ahmad), parent(X,muhammad).
X = ali.

Explanation:
This query looks for a person who is a parent of both Ahmad and Muhammad. The result identifies Ali as their shared parent.

This query verifies that Khan is male and that Khan and Nora have the same parent. Prolog identifies Ahmad as their common parent, which supports the brother relationship.
